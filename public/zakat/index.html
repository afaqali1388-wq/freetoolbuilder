Questions people ask

How much zakat do I pay?

Zakat is 2.5% of your zakatable wealth (cash, gold, silver, savings and trade goods minus debts due now), if it is above the nisab for one lunar year.

What is the nisab for zakat?

The nisab is the value of 87.48 grams (7.5 tola) of gold or 612.36 grams (52.5 tola) of silver. Enter today's price and the calculator does the rest.

Should I use the gold or silver nisab?

Many scholars advise the silver nisab because it is lower and so helps more people in need. Ask your scholar if you are not sure.
