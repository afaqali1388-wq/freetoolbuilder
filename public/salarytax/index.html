Questions people ask

What is the tax-free salary limit in Pakistan for 2026-27?

Salary up to Rs 600,000 a year (Rs 50,000 a month) has no income tax for Tax Year 2027.

What are the salaried tax slabs for 2026-27?

There are eight slabs: 0% up to Rs 600,000, then 1%, 11%, 20%, 25%, 29%, 32% and 35% on income above Rs 7 million, with a fixed amount for each slab.

Is the 9% surcharge still charged on salaries?

No. Finance Act 2026 removed the surcharge for salaried people, so the calculator does not add it.

Are my files or details uploaded?

No. The tool runs in your browser, so what you type and the files you open stay on your device.
