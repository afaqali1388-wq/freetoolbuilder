Questions people ask

Can I change existing text?

You can cover it with white-out and type new text on top.

Is the pdf editor free?

Yes. It is free to use on FreeToolBuilder, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Are my files or details uploaded?

No. The tool runs in your browser, so what you type and the files you open stay on your device.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It works in the browser on Android phones, iPhones, tablets and computers, and you can add FreeToolBuilder to your home screen.
