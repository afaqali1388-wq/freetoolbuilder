Questions people ask

Do the QR codes expire?

No. They are static codes, so they work forever.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Add a logo and choose your colors. The tool keeps the code scannable.

Can I make a Wi-Fi QR code?

Yes. Enter the network name and password and guests can join by scanning.

Are my files or details uploaded?

No. The tool runs in your browser, so what you type and the files you open stay on your device.
